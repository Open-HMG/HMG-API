# Supporting HMG-API

HMG-API and its mods are free, and they're made by volunteers in their own time. If
HMG-API saved you a dealer visit or made your drives nicer, you can support the people
who build it.

Each maintainer has their own links, and a donation goes straight to the person you pick:
there's no shared pot.

## Other ways to help

Money isn't the only way, and often not the most useful one:

- **Send a [Car Report](Car-Report)** for your car, especially on a firmware or model
  that isn't mapped yet. Every new car starts with one.
- **Test on your car** and say how it went, on the pull request or issue: which car,
  which head unit software, what worked and what didn't.
- **Help other owners** in the [issues](https://github.com/Open-HMG/HMG-API/issues).
- **Write a mod**, or fix one: see [Making mods](Making-mods).
